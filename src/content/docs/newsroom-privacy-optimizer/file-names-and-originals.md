In **Red Jaguar Newsroom → Settings → Files that give you away**.

## File names

A name like `PXL_20261028_091500.jpg` or `WhatsApp Image…` tells which device took the photo, the exact time, or how it arrived. It becomes the public address of the image.

**Rename files named by phones, cameras and apps** replaces those names in the file, the title and the address of the attachment page. Names you write yourself are kept.

**The new name:**

- **The title of the article:** recommended. A photo uploaded in an article titled "March in the main square" becomes `march-in-the-main-square.jpg`, which also helps image search. Images uploaded outside an article get a neutral name.
- **Always a neutral name:** like `image-k3x9ab.jpg`. For confidential stories: an uploaded file can be opened by anyone who knows its address, even before the article is published.

## Full-size original

For photos over 2,560 pixels, iPhone photos (HEIC) and photos published as WebP, WordPress publishes a smaller copy but keeps the full original on the server, where anyone who finds its address can download it.

**Delete the original of large photos after WordPress makes its published copy** deletes it once the published copy exists and is clean.

:::note
Without the original, WordPress cannot make sizes larger than the published copy later.
:::

If another plugin moves your media to cloud storage, originals are kept, because the plugin cannot check the copies stored elsewhere.

For originals of images that were already there, see [Your media library](/newsroom-privacy-optimizer/media-library/#delete-the-originals).
