**Red Jaguar Newsroom Privacy & Optimizer** removes hidden GPS, device and owner data from every photo before publishing, and helps you find and clean the images already in your media library. Everything happens on your own server.

It is **free, forever, updates included**, and it is published on [WordPress.org](https://wordpress.org/plugins/redjaguar-newsroom-privacy-optimizer/). It needs no license and no account.

In the WordPress menu it appears as **Red Jaguar Newsroom**.

## Why it matters

Photos carry data you cannot see: GPS coordinates, the serial number of the phone or camera, the owner's name, the date and time, sometimes a small copy of the uncropped photo. Anyone who downloads an image from your site can read it. See [About photo metadata](/newsroom-privacy-optimizer/photo-metadata/).

## What it does

- **[Cleans every new image](/newsroom-privacy-optimizer/new-images/)** you upload, before WordPress makes its sizes.
- **[Replaces file names](/newsroom-privacy-optimizer/file-names-and-originals/)** that give away the device or the time, and deletes the full-size originals WordPress keeps.
- **[Reviews and cleans your media library](/newsroom-privacy-optimizer/media-library/)**, the images that were already there.
- **[Makes images lighter](/newsroom-privacy-optimizer/lighter-images/)** with WebP or AVIF, as an optional extra.

## What it does not do

- It does not change what you can see in a photo: faces, plates, signs. Blur or crop them before publishing.
- It does not clean PDFs, videos, audio or documents, nor HEIC, AVIF or GIF images yet.
- It cannot reach copies outside your server: images already shared, sent by email, stored by a CDN or indexed by search engines.
