Paying newsrooms fund donated licenses for independent and at-risk media. They are endorsed by [chequea.la](https://chequea.la/), [seguridades.org](https://seguridades.org/), [somosversatiles.org](https://somosversatiles.org/) and other allied organizations.

**The criteria and the decision on each request depend on those allied organizations**, not on Red Jaguar.

## Ask for one

Fill in the form at [redjaguar.agency/plugins/donated-license](https://redjaguar.agency/plugins/donated-license/).

If the request is approved, you get an email with a link to accept the conditions of the donated license. After you accept them, you get your key and an account, like any other customer. From there, everything works as described in this manual.
