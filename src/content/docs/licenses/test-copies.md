You can try changes on copies of your site without buying more licenses.

## Test copies of your domain

A license activated for `example.org` also works on:

- `staging.example.org`
- `dev.example.org`
- `test.example.org`

On those copies, the License screen says "Development or staging copy: it does not use up the license."

## Copies on your computer

These need **no license at all**:

- `localhost` and `127.0.0.1`
- any address ending in `.local`, `.test` or `.localhost`

## Other addresses

A copy on any other address (for example `example.hostingcompany.com` or `staging-example.org`) counts as another site and needs its own license. If your hosting provider gives your test copy an address like that, ask them whether you can use one of the addresses above instead.
