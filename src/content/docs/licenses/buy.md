## Choose a plan

Go to [redjaguar.agency/plugins](https://redjaguar.agency/plugins/), open the plugin and choose a plan:

- **1 site:** one license.
- **5 sites:** five licenses, one for each site. Useful for networks or newsrooms with several outlets.

Prices are shown on the pricing page. They are yearly and renew by themselves until you cancel.

:::tip[Independent or at-risk newsroom?]
You may be able to get a donated license. See [Donated licenses](/licenses/donated-licenses/).
:::

## Pay

The buy button takes you to a payment page by Stripe, our payment provider. You can pay with a card. Red Jaguar never sees your card number.

If you have a discount code, write it on the Stripe payment page.

## After paying

You return to a page that says **"We received your payment. Thank you!"**. In a few minutes you get **two emails** at the address you used to pay:

1. **Your license keys:** one for each site where you install the plugin.
2. **Your username and a link to create your password.** You can also log in with your email address.

If you already had an account with that email address, you will not get the second email: the new licenses are already in your account.

:::note[Nothing after a few minutes?]
Check your spam folder. If it is not there, write to [roar@redjaguar.agency](mailto:roar@redjaguar.agency) from the email address you used to pay.
:::
