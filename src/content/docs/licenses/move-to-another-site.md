title: Move a license to another site
description: Change the domain where a license is activated.

---

A license works on one domain at a time. To use it on another site, release it first.

## From the old site

1. Open the License screen of the plugin on the old site.
2. Click **Release this site**.

Once released, the plugin is locked on that site until a license is activated again. **No data is deleted.**

## From your account

If the old site is down or you no longer have access to it:

1. Log in to [your account](https://redjaguar.agency/plugins/account/).
2. In the license, click **Release the domain**.

## Then, on the new site

Paste the key in the License screen and click **Activate on this site**.

:::note
The domain of a license can change **once every 24 hours**. If it is an emergency, write to [roar@redjaguar.agency](mailto:roar@redjaguar.agency).
:::

## Moving the same site to a new domain

If you move your site to a new domain (for example, from `oldname.org` to `newname.org`), the license stays activated for the old domain and the plugin locks itself on the new one, with a message that the license is activated for another domain.

1. Before the move, click **Release this site** in the License screen. If the site already moved, click **Release the domain** in your account instead.
2. On the new domain, open the License screen and click **Activate on this site**.
