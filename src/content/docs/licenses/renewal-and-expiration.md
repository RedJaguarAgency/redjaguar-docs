## Renewal

Licenses last one year and **renew by themselves** with the card you paid with. Stripe emails you a reminder before each renewal.

To change the card, download invoices or **cancel the renewal**, log in to [your account](https://redjaguar.agency/plugins/account/) and click **Card, invoices and renewal**.

If you cancel, the license stays active until the end of the year you paid.

## When a license expires

The plugin **keeps working** with everything you have. Only these stop:

- updates, including security fixes;
- support.

The License screen says "Expired: the plugin keeps working, without updates. Renew it from your account."

You can renew at any time from your account.

## When a license is cancelled

A license is cancelled after a refund or a chargeback, or if it is misused. Then the plugin is locked on that site, as if it had no license. Your settings and data are kept: activating another license brings everything back.

See the [refund policy](https://redjaguar.agency/plugins/refunds/) and the [terms of sale](https://redjaguar.agency/plugins/terms/).

## "The license could not be checked with the store for more than 14 days"

The plugin asks the store about the license once a day. If your server cannot reach redjaguar.agency for 14 days, the plugin locks itself until it can check again.

1. Ask your hosting provider whether the server can make outgoing connections to redjaguar.agency.
2. Open the License screen of the plugin and click **Check now**.
