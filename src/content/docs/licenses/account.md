Your account is at [redjaguar.agency/plugins/account](https://redjaguar.agency/plugins/account/). Log in with your username or with the email address you used to pay. If you do not remember the password, click **Forgot your password?**.

## What you see

Each license shows:

- the last characters of its key ("Key ending in …");
- until when it includes updates and support;
- the site where it is activated, or "Not activated on any site".

## What you can do

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Download the latest version** | Downloads the plugin zip. Needs an active license. |
| **Release the domain** | Frees the license so you can activate it on another site. See [Move a license to another site](/licenses/move-to-another-site/). |
| **I lost my key: make a new one** | Creates a new key and shows it. The old key stops working: paste the new one in the plugin's License screen. |
| **Card, invoices and renewal** | Opens the billing page by Stripe: change the card, download invoices or cancel the yearly renewal. |

:::caution[After making a new key]
The site that used the old key stops getting updates until you paste the new key in its License screen and click **Activate on this site**.
:::
