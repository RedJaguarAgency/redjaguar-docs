title: Support
description: How to ask for help with a Red Jaguar plugin.
---

Write to **[roar@redjaguar.agency](mailto:roar@redjaguar.agency)**. We answer in English and Spanish.

Support is included while your license is active. Free plugins are supported on a best-effort basis.

## What to include

So we can help on the first reply, tell us:

- the plugin and its version (in **Plugins**, under its name);
- the domain of the site;
- what you did, what you expected and what happened instead;
- the exact text of any message, or a screenshot.

The WordPress screen **Tools → Site Health → Info** has a **Copy site info to clipboard** button. Pasting that in your email answers most of our questions.

:::caution[Never send]
Passwords, your full license key or private data from your site. The last characters of the key are enough: your account shows them as "Key ending in …".
:::

## Security problems

If you found a security problem in a plugin, write to the same address and do not post it publicly until it is fixed.
