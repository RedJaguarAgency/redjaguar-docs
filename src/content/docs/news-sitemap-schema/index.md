**Red Jaguar - News Sitemap & Schema** checks every day the technical requirements Google sets for news sites, fixes what it can for you and guides you step by step through the rest. It was built for small and independent newsrooms, including those that work under pressure, so it also checks what the site reveals about the people who make it.

In the WordPress menu it appears as **News Schema**.

## What it does

- **[News sitemap](/news-sitemap-schema/news-sitemap/):** the list of your latest articles in the format Google News reads.
- **[Structured data](/news-sitemap-schema/structured-data/):** tells search engines that each article is news, who wrote it and which newsroom publishes it. Works with Yoast SEO and Rank Math.
- **[Readiness check](/news-sitemap-schema/readiness-check/):** about 80 checks, every day, against the pages your site really publishes. Each problem comes with how to fix it.
- **[Newsroom protection](/news-sitemap-schema/newsroom-protection/):** finds and fixes what can expose your team: photos with their location, addresses that reveal login names, the public list of users.
- **[Authors](/news-sitemap-schema/authors/):** job title, biography and profiles for each author, and a newsroom account for unsigned articles.
- **[Checks before publishing](/news-sitemap-schema/block-editor/):** in the block editor, the featured image, its credit and the author's biography.

## What it cannot do

- **It cannot get your site into Google News.** Google decides which sites appear, and there is no form to apply. The plugin takes care of the technical part and tells you what else Google looks for.
- **It does not write your editorial policies.** It checks which ones you have and adds them to the structured data. What they say is up to your newsroom.
- **It does not see Google Search Console.** What only Google knows is in the [manual checks](/news-sitemap-schema/readiness-check/#manual-checks).

## Requirements

- WordPress 6.5 or later and PHP 7.4 or later.
- A [license](/licenses/) activated for the site's domain.
