In the block editor, the article settings panel has a **Red Jaguar - News Sitemap & Schema** section.

## News check

Before publishing, it reviews:

- **The featured image:** whether there is one, and whether it is large enough. Google Discover needs it at least 1,200 pixels wide for large previews.
- **Its alternative text.**
- **Its credit**, from the "Credit" field of the media library.
- **The author's biography.**

When everything is in order it says "News check: all good". These checks do not block publishing; they help the article stand out in Google News and Discover.

## Exclude from the news sitemap

Check **Exclude from the news sitemap** to leave this article out. Otherwise it is listed in the news sitemap for 48 hours after publication.

## Article type

**Article type** is "Automatic" by default, which follows the [category settings](/news-sitemap-schema/structured-data/#article-type-by-category). Change it for a single article, for example an opinion piece in a news category.

## Photo credit

Each image in the media library has a **Credit** field. Write the name of the photographer or agency there; it is published as the credit of the photo in the structured data.
