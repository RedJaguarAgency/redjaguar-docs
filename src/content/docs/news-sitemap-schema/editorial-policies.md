Public policies show readers and search engines who is behind your journalism and how you work. Google uses them to judge whether a source is trustworthy.

## The policies

The seven **key policies** count in the setup progress on the Dashboard:

| Policy | What it explains |
|---|---|
| About us | Who you are, where you report from and since when. |
| Masthead | The editorial team and who is responsible for what. |
| Editorial standards | The principles your newsroom follows when it reports and publishes. |
| Corrections | How readers can report an error and how you correct it. |
| Ethics and conflicts of interest | Gifts, outside work, political activity and how you handle conflicts. |
| Ownership and funding | Who owns the outlet, how it is funded and how you protect editorial independence. |
| Contact and right of reply | How to reach the newsroom, send tips or request a reply. |

The screen also lists other policies you can add: verification, anonymous sources, bylines and pseudonyms, mission and coverage priorities, use of artificial intelligence, advertising and sponsored content, comments and moderation, terms of use and diversity.

## Assign your policies

1. Write each policy in a page of your site.
2. Open **News Schema → Policies**.
3. For each policy, choose its page and click **Save assignments**.

Published policies are added to the structured data of your newsroom.

The **Status** column tells you how each one is:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Missing** | No page assigned. |
| **Draft** | The page is not published yet. |
| **Incomplete** | The page still has guide text from a template. |
| **Review (over a year old)** | Published, but not updated in more than a year. |
| **Published** | Ready. |

The privacy policy is managed by WordPress in **Settings → Privacy**.

## Start from a template

For a missing policy, click **Create draft from template**. The plugin creates a draft page with the structure of that policy, in the language of your site (Spanish or English; any other language gets the English one).

:::caution[Adapt it before publishing]
Templates only give you the structure. A policy copied without adapting it does more harm than good. Replace every text between `[✎ …]` with how your newsroom really works. Pages that still contain that guide text are marked as **Incomplete** and are not added to the structured data.
:::
