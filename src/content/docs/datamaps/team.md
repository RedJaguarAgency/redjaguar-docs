## Roles

In **Users**, give each person a role:

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Administrator** | Everything: fields, geography, views, forms, settings and every dataset. |
| **Editor** | The same as a DataMaps reviewer. |
| **DataMaps reviewer** | Add, edit, publish and verify records. |
| **DataMaps data entry** | Add and edit records. What they add waits for review, and a published record they change leaves the site until someone reviews it. |

## Who works on each dataset

In **Settings → Team** of a dataset, check who works on it. With nobody checked, everyone listed works on it. With some checked, only they see it. Administrators see every dataset.
