## In a page of your site

Use any of these, and choose the view:

- **Block editor:** the **DataMaps** block.
- **Elementor:** the **DataMaps** widget.
- **Shortcode:** `[datamaps view="3"]`. Each view shows its shortcode in the Views tab. Add `align="wide"` or `align="full"` to make it wider.

Views of the same dataset on one page share their filters, and the page address keeps the filters chosen.

## A ready monitor page

**Views → Create a monitor page → Create page** builds a cover, filters, figures, charts, map, cards, table and form from your public fields, in a **draft** page. Adjust it before publishing.

## Show in another site

*Video in Spanish.*

**Views → Show in another site** gives a code to paste in another website, for example an allied outlet: an HTML block in WordPress, or an embed in other platforms.

1. Choose the views, in order.
2. Check the **Preview**.
3. Click **Copy code** and paste it in the other site.

The other site shows the views with the data of your site, always up to date. It shows only published records and public fields, and gets nothing from your dashboard.
