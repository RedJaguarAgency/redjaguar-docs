**Red Jaguar DataMaps → Style** sets the look of every view, unless a view picks its own.

- **Themes:** dark editorial, light, institutional or high contrast, or your own colors (background, regions, borders, accent, text and secondary text).
- **Map background:** soft glow, dot texture or flat color.
- **Font and size of the numbers.** Texts use the fonts of your theme. Fonts are not loaded from other servers: if the one you pick is not on the site, a similar one is used.
- **Add "DataMaps · Red Jaguar" to the credits of the maps:** optional, off by default.

The preview shows one of your views and changes as you pick. Nothing changes on the site until you save.

In a view, editing a color switches it to custom colors. **Edit the site style** takes you back here.
