The **Records** tab lists the records of the dataset. Click **Add record** to add one by hand, or **Save and add another** to keep going.

## Status of a record

| Status | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Only the team. Imports can create drafts so you review them first. |
| **Pending review** | Only the team. Reports from forms and records added by data entry people wait here. |
| **Published** | Everyone: it shows in the views, with its public fields only. |

The DataMaps menu and the **Overview** tab show how many records are waiting for review.

## Review a record

- **Mark verified:** for records whose sources your team checked.
- **Review note (private):** sources checked, doubts, who confirmed it. It is encrypted when the site has an encryption key.
- **Possible duplicate:** DataMaps warns you when other records have the same date and place, and links to them.
- **History:** every change to the record, with who made it and when.

Each field shows whether it is public or private, and whether it is encrypted.

## Columns of the list

Click **Columns** to choose up to 6 fields to show, in order. Date, place, verification and status are always shown. Only you see this choice.

## Export

- **Export all (CSV):** every record and field, for your team. It holds private data: keep the file safe.
- **Export public data (CSV):** only published records and public fields.

Exports are protected against formula injection when opened in a spreadsheet.

## Limit

Up to 20,000 records per dataset, so public pages stay fast on phones.
