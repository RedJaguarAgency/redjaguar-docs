## 1.0.1

- Link fields (hero button, stats link, methodology, license) accept addresses, paths of the site (`/data/`) and anchors (`#data`).
- Links to the site itself are saved without the domain, so they keep working when the site moves to another domain. Sites updated from 1.0.0 are converted automatically.
- The "change" figure compares the current year with the same period of the previous year (up to the date of the latest record) and says so.
- The trend arrow of the change figure points down when the change is negative.
