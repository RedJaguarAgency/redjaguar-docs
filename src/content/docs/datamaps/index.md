**Red Jaguar - DataMaps** turns a WordPress site into a data monitor: a database with your own fields, kept by your team, published as an interactive map with figures, charts, filters and tables that update by themselves.

It was made for newsrooms and organizations that document what official figures leave out (violence, attacks on the press, environmental conflicts, internet shutdowns), and it works for any data with a place and a date.

In the WordPress menu it appears as **Red Jaguar DataMaps**.

## What it does

- **[Datasets](/datamaps/datasets/) with your own [fields](/datamaps/fields/)**, from scratch or from a template.
- **Fill them** by hand, by [importing a spreadsheet](/datamaps/import/), by [syncing a Google Sheet](/datamaps/google-sheets/) or with [public forms](/datamaps/forms/).
- **Review them:** [data quality](/datamaps/quality/), verification, private notes and the history of every change.
- **Publish them** as [views](/datamaps/views/): cover, map, figures, charts, filters, timeline, cards and table, in any page or [in another site](/datamaps/place-views/#show-in-another-site).

## Privacy comes first

Every field starts private. Exact coordinates are never published, small places are protected, and private values can be encrypted. See [Privacy and security](/datamaps/privacy-and-security/).

## Requirements

- WordPress 6.5 or later and PHP 7.4 or later, with the `sodium` and `zip` extensions (almost every host has them).
- A [license](/licenses/) activated for the site's domain.
